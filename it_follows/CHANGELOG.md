# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-16

- Horde mode: the game slows down less when the horde is big. The mod's work is lighter and better spread out.
- New: the Hardcore in the Horde mode, always on. The enemies grow 1% stronger every 100 enemies killed during the
  run, and the percent shows in the in-game bar. It starts again from 0% at each run.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-15

- First version: the Horde mode (beta), the normal mode with its three levels, and its Hardcore option.
