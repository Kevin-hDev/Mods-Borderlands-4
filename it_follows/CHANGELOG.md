# Changelog

## 1.0.8 - 2026-10-19

- Options: new "Lost Loot: 400 items" option, the Lost Loot machine keeps up to 400 items instead of the game's limit.
- Options: new "16:10 resolution" option, to play in Fullscreen at the 16:10 resolution of your screen's height.
- Options: new "Color profile" option, brighter colors over the whole game, with three profiles and a preview to try them in game.

## 1.0.7 - 2026-10-19

- Horde mode: new Starting point option, to start your run from one of seven places on the map.
- Normal mode: enemies stagger less, and at Predator a new No staggers option takes their staggers away.

## 1.0.6 - 2026-10-18

- Normal mode now works in co-op.

## 1.0.5 - 2026-10-17

- Normal mode: in combat, the enemies strike, leap and throw their weapons faster, a little more at each level.
- Normal mode: at Senior and Predator, armed bandits wait less between two melee blows.

## 1.0.4 - 2026-10-17

- Normal mode: more experience from the enemies you kill, more ammo and money picked up, depending on the level.
- No change for vending machines and missions.
- Translation: Simplified Chinese.

## 1.0.3 - 2026-10-16

- Normal mode: at Senior and Predator, the enemies run a little slower than before.
- Normal mode: new "Enemy speed" setting at Senior and Predator. REDUCED makes the enemies 15% slower, when running and
  in their faster dodges.
- Normal mode's Hardcore: only the enemies your Vault Hunter kills count, not those your companions kill.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-10-16

- Normal mode: enemies without a shield get one, 40% of their health, bosses included. At Bandit, an option takes them
  away. At Predator, a broken shield recharges 10 seconds later.
- Normal mode: armed enemies shoot straighter and their bullets fly faster, a little more at each level.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-16

- Horde mode: the game slows down less when the horde is big. The mod's work is lighter and better spread out.
- New: the Hardcore in the Horde mode, always on. The enemies grow 1% stronger every 100 enemies killed during the
  run, and the percent shows in the in-game bar. It starts again from 0% at each run.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-15

- First version: the Horde mode (beta), the normal mode with its three levels, and its Hardcore option.
