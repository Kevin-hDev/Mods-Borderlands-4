# Changelog

## 1.0.4 - 2026-10-04

- Add a VEHICLES page with separate buttons for 10 standard vehicles, 4 promotional vehicles, and Trident with its rewards.
- Promotional vehicles remain available through reloads and restarts while enabled. After disabling and re-enabling the mod, another unlock click may be needed.

## 1.0.3 - 2026-10-05

- New Reverse speed setting: the vehicle backs up faster (200 % by default, up to 300 %).
- New Boost page:
  - Boost duration: the boost gauge lasts longer (150 % by default, up to 300 %).
  - Unlimited boost: the gauge never empties (off by default).
  - Boost in the air: the boost keeps pushing you forward in jumps and falls, up to about its top speed on the ground
    (100 % by default, 0 to 300 %).
- New Combat page:
  - Toughness: the vehicle takes less damage (200 % by default, so half the damage, up to 500 %).
  - Weapon damage: the machine gun and the rockets hit harder (300 % by default, up to 500 %).
- At 100 % (0 % for Boost in the air), a new setting gives the game's own behavior. Your current settings are kept.
- Internal update of the settings window shared with the Apex mods.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-09-25

- Grip: no more sudden jolt when it takes over again after a pause or when you switch it back on in the menu.
- Internal update of the settings window shared with the Apex mods.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-09-23

- Add the same visual settings window as Apex Movement and Apex Grapple, with English and French labels, controller
  navigation, undo after a reset, and saved section selection. It stays responsive while the game is paused.
- Shorter setting descriptions, in English and French: each one now says what you get in a single plain sentence.
- Setting names, values, limits and driving behavior are unchanged.

## 1.0.0 — first public release

Livelier vehicles, one setting per effect: max speed, acceleration, turn speed, jump height, and a grip that keeps
the vehicle going where it faces through turns, with the share of speed it lets go in a 90 degree turn.

Built and tested on game version 1.8.1-4709277, single player, with a controller, on three different vehicles.
