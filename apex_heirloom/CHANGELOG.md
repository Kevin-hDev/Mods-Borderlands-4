# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-09-29

- New heirloom: an axe, with its own animations and a glowing blade.
- Choose the Jakobs knife or the axe on the HEIRLOOM page.
- Eight skins for the axe, chosen with two arrows. Each heirloom keeps its own skin and size.
- The glow of the axe's blade is adjustable from 1 to 5.
- Fixed: the heirloom looked blurry, close to your eyes.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-27

- Initial release.
- An Apex Legends style heirloom: with no weapon in hand, a Jakobs knife in the right hand with its own animations.
- Apex mode, with all the heirloom's animations, or Borderlands mode, with ours at rest, walking and running.
- Adjustable knife size, and the moment and speed of its draw.
- A key to put your weapon away, held or pressed, on keyboard and on controller.
- A settings window in English and French, with HEIRLOOM, HOLSTER and CONTROLS pages.
- Two separate files as well: Tidy Weapons, the holster alone, and Heirloom, the heirloom alone.
