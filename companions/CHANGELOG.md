# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-23

- Your team's Vault Hunters now show their name under their health bar when you aim at them, in green like the game's allies. Update the Companions Paks file too.
- Fix: about twenty minutes after a team was stopped, its companions could come back on their own where they had appeared, the Vault Hunters looking like Lemmy, standing still and not following you. They no longer come back.
- If you also have It Follows, update it to 1.0.12: with both mods enabled, It Follows is the one that brings the team.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-23

- First version: a team of up to 8 companions, among the 6 Vault Hunters and 4 allies, who follow you and fight at your side, without It Follows. Its window is in English, French, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.
