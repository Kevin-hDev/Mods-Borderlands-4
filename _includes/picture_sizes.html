{%- comment -%} The srcset and sizes of one of the site's pictures, written after its src: its WebP copies, listed in _data/picture_sizes.json by borderlands_4/outils/site_picture_sizes.py, and the width the picture takes in its place, which the browser needs to choose before the page is laid out. These widths follow site.css and recommended.css (--text-width, --page-width, --nav-width, the gutters, the cards' grid and the 760 px step): change them together. A width a little too large only loads a bigger copy. A picture without copies keeps its JPEG alone. include.file: the picture's path from the site's root, without the first /. include.place: text (a page without the list of pages), beside-pages (a Mod Settings page), card or thumb (one of three pictures in a card). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ps_entry = site.data.picture_sizes.pictures[include.file] -%} {%- if ps_entry -%} {%- assign ps_cut = include.file.size | minus: 4 -%} {%- assign ps_stem = include.file | slice: 0, ps_cut | prepend: "/" -%} {%- case include.place -%} {%- when "card" -%}{%- assign ps_sizes = "(max-width: 600px) calc(100vw - 32px), 400px" -%} {%- when "thumb" -%}{%- assign ps_sizes = "(max-width: 600px) calc((100vw - 32px) / 3), 134px" -%} {%- when "beside-pages" -%}{%- assign ps_sizes = "(max-width: 760px) calc(100vw - 32px), 780px" -%} {%- else -%}{%- assign ps_sizes = "(max-width: 760px) calc(100vw - 32px), (max-width: 1060px) calc(100vw - 56px), 1004px" -%} {%- endcase %} srcset="{% for ps_width in ps_entry.widths %}{{ ps_stem | append: "-" | append: ps_width | append: ".webp" | relative_url }} {{ ps_width }}w{% unless forloop.last %}, {% endunless %}{% endfor %}" sizes="{{ ps_sizes }}" {%- endif -%}
