{%- comment -%} French puts a space before ; : ? ! » and after «. A space that never breaks keeps the sign on the line of its word, instead of alone at the start of the next one. The French pages' code samples have none of these, checked on 2026-10-27: a code sample written with one would be changed too. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.lang == "fr" -%} {{ include.text | replace: " ;", " ;" | replace: " :", " :" | replace: " ?", " ?" | replace: " !", " !" | replace: "« ", "« " | replace: " »", " »" }} {%- else -%} {{ include.text }} {%- endif -%}
