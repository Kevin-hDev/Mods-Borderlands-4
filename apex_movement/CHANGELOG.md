# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release

The eight moves listed in the README, each with its own switch and settings:
auto sprint, momentum slides, the Axle slide, a longer dash, air crouch, air
strafe, heavier falls and the wall climb.

Built and tested on game version 1.8.1-4709277, in the first area of the game,
single player, with a controller and with mouse and keyboard.
