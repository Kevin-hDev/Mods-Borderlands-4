# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-17

- Duels of 2 to 4 players, each for themselves or in teams.
- New TEAMS page in the window: the host puts each player in team 1, 2, 3 or 4.
- A player who is down stays on the ground until the round ends, and their teammates can get them back up.
- The round goes to the last team standing, and the score shows each team.
- Tested with two players only.
