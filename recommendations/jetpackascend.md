# Jetpack Ascend

Jetpack Ascend lets you actually go up with the glide pack, the game's jetpack, instead of only gliding. Look up to climb; look straight ahead or down and you glide as usual. It is not overpowered: just a more comfortable way of getting around.

![A Vault Hunter climbing in the air with the glide pack, its gauge at the bottom of the screen](images/jetpackascend/climbing.jpg){: width="1600" height="1296"}

## What it does

- **Look up to climb.** While gliding, aim above the horizon and the glide pack takes you upward.
- **Glide as usual otherwise.** Look straight ahead or down and the glide works as in the base game.
- **It still runs out.** The glide pack keeps its gauge: climbing uses it up like gliding does, and the mod does not change how fast it drains.
- **Co-op.** The mod works in co-op, and only the host needs it.

## Settings

Two settings are in the mod's options:

- **Ascend Speed**: how fast you climb, from 200 to 3,000 (900 by default; 100 is about one metre per second).
- **Look-Up Threshold (degrees)**: how far above the horizon you must aim before you climb instead of gliding, from 0 to 80 degrees (15 by default).

## Installation

Jetpack Ascend needs the Oak2 Mod Manager: [installation guide](https://bl-sdk.github.io/oak2-mod-db/).

1. Install the Oak2 Mod Manager.
2. Download Jetpack Ascend from its Nexus page, and place its `.sdkmod` file in the game's `sdk_mods` folder (`...\Borderlands 4\sdk_mods`).
3. Run the game.
4. Press the console key twice (the key under Esc on a US keyboard) to open the console, type `mods`, then press Enter.
5. Press the number shown next to JetpackAscend.
