# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-02

- Initial release.
- Hold a key: your left hand rises and fires a beam at what you aim at, in first and third person.
- Six elements: fire, shock, corrosive, cryo, radiation, or kinetic.
- Damage that follows your level.
- Its own energy and its bar under your stamina bar.
- A settings window in English and French; the keyboard key and the controller button are yours to choose.
